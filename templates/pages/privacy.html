Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026

This explains what coroutines collects about you, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we collect what's needed to run a social network, we don't sell it, and you can delete everything yourself.

What we collect

Account details: your email address, handle, display name, bio and a hashed version of your password (we never store the password itself).

Things you create: posts, replies, reposts, likes, bookmarks, who you follow, who you block, and direct messages.

Technical data: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for a short period to keep the service running and secure.

Preferences: a cookie remembering your light/dark theme choice.

How we use it

To run the service, show your content to other people, send you account emails such as the confirmation link when you sign up, keep the site safe from abuse, and respond when you contact us. We don't show ads and we don't sell or rent your data.

Who can see what

Your profile, posts, replies, reposts and follow lists are public. Likes are shown as counts. Bookmarks are private to you. Direct messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation; the site operator can access them only to investigate abuse reports or when required by law.

Services we rely on

Your data is processed by Render (hosting) and Supabase (database), both in Singapore, and account emails are sent through Google. Each acts under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in, a security cookie to prevent forged requests, and a cookie for your theme preference. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can edit your profile and delete individual posts at any time. Deleting your account from Settings removes your account, posts, replies, likes, bookmarks, follows and messages immediately. Copies may remain in backups for up to 30 days before being purged.

Children

coroutines is not for anyone under 13. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top. Questions or requests about your data: atatat6659@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
